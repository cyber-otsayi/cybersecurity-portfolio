# Social Media Safety Research — Nigeria

## Overview

A final-year cybersecurity research project examining safety-related features and claims of social media platforms, with the research scope narrowed to Nigeria.

The project connects cybersecurity, platform governance, user protection, privacy, and regulatory/compliance considerations.

## Research Focus

- Social media safety mechanisms
- Platform security and user protection
- Privacy and security controls
- Cybersecurity governance
- Regulatory and compliance considerations
- Platform communication and safety claims

## Scope

The research applies a Nigeria-focused perspective to the assessment of social media safety and security practices.

## Methodology

The detailed methodology, coding framework, sampling approach, datasets, findings, and analysis will be documented here using the final approved research materials.

## Why This Matters

Social media platforms operate at the intersection of technology, cybersecurity, privacy, governance, and regulation. Studying their safety mechanisms provides a useful foundation for understanding how technical controls and governance requirements can work together to protect users.
